Made for how business is done here

Sharp Desk makes practical tools for Nigerian small businesses: printable forms for the counter or the fitting room, and simple trackers that open in Google Sheets on a phone.

Each tool starts with a problem owners describe to us, such as an unpaid balance, a measurement nobody wrote down, or stock that runs out unnoticed. If a form or a tab does not help someone get paid, deliver on time or know what they have, it does not go in.

  1. Start with the ownerWe talk to people who run the business and ask how they keep records today.
  2. Build around one real orderThe forms and the tracker follow a single job from first enquiry to payment.
  3. Test it with a sample customerWe run a pretend customer through every form and formula before anything is sold.
  4. Fix what confuses peopleWorking owners try it, and their feedback changes the product.

Before you buy

How do I get my download?

Pay on Selar's secure checkout. The files are available to download straight after payment.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The tracker opens in Google Sheets on a phone, and the forms are PDFs you can fill on screen or print.

Do I have to print everything?

No. Print only the forms you want on paper. The tracker stays on your phone or laptop.
