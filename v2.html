Frequently asked questions

Is this a physical product?

No. This is a digital download. You receive a ZIP file containing the printable PDF forms and the tracker workbook (.xlsx), which you can open in Google Sheets or Microsoft Excel.

Can I use the forms without the Google Sheets / Excel tracker?

Yes. The printable PDF forms are designed to be useful on their own. The Google Sheets / Excel tracker is an additional tool for anyone who wants to keep digital records.

What do I need to use the files?

You can open the .xlsx tracker in Google Sheets, including on your phone, or use Microsoft Excel. You need a device that can open PDF files to view or print the forms. Print the forms on A4 paper.

Can I customise the forms?

You can add your business name to the forms before printing and write in any additional garment-specific details you need. The measurement fields are a general starting point.

Does this kit teach tailoring or garment construction?

No. It is a record-keeping and order-management resource. It does not replace your professional measurement methods, fitting process or tailoring expertise.
